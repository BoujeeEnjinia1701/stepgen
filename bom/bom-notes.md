# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers 1 to 12 match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`); item 13 covers fasteners and consumables.

Parts total about $397, just inside the $400 concept budget in `project.yaml`. There is almost no margin for spares, shipping or a second attempt at the frame or flywheel. Salvaged bicycle parts (freewheels, chain, sprockets) and a salvaged motor could bring the total down by $50 to $100. Raising the budget is not proposed at this stage; if TRL 3 pricing pushes the total over $400, a change will be proposed in `docs/REVIEW.md` for Amish to decide.

The PowerBox (storage) is a separate portfolio project and is not in this BOM.
